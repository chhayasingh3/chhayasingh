Profile

About Me

I am an academic and researcher working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science and intelligent computational modelling. My research journey spans bioinformatics, healthcare, IoT, sensor networks, cyber-physical systems, fault diagnosis and privacy-preserving distributed learning.

My doctoral work focused on soft and fuzzy-set-based machine learning approaches for conformational epitope prediction. My recent research extends into deep learning, federated learning, privacy-aware AI and intelligent decision-support systems. I also contribute through invited lectures, academic training, conference leadership, reviewing and technical-program service.
